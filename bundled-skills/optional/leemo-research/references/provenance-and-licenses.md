# Provenance and license boundaries

## Clean-core policy

This skill is an original control plane distilled from an archived tutorial collection plus independent workflow design. The source archive remains outside this distributable folder. Marketing copy, seller lead generation, authenticated download URLs, outcome guarantees, detector-evasion prompts, plagiarism-oriented rewriting, and unverified “installed skill” claims are not included.

No third-party skill implementation is bundled in the core. Generic research practices—traceable search, screening reasons, evidence tables, claim support, experiment logs, independent review, and legal access—are expressed as new host-neutral contracts and templates.

The owner of Leemo Research has expressly authorized this package to be bundled and distributed with Leemo. This project-specific authorization is not an open-source license and does not grant third parties a general right to extract, relicense, or redistribute Leemo Research as a standalone package. Referenced repositories and optional adapters remain governed by their own licenses and are not covered by this authorization.

## Adapter intake record

Before installing, copying, modifying, or redistributing an external capability, record:

- repository and exact commit/tag;
- selected files/skill names;
- license file and any per-file exception;
- dependencies, scripts, network calls, and required secrets;
- data egress and filesystem scope;
- modifications and attribution requirements;
- internal use versus redistribution/commercial use;
- test evidence and uninstall/rollback path.

Popularity, star count, a tutorial recommendation, or a zip attachment is not security, compatibility, quality, or license evidence.
